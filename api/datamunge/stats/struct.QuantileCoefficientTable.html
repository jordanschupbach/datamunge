    QuantileCoefficientTable

    struct QuantileCoefficientTable
    #include <datamunge/stats/quantile_regression.hpp> source

    Coefficients with standard errors, t statistics, p-values and confidence intervals, from QuantileRegression::coefficient_table().

    The t statistics are referred to Student's t with \(n-p\) degrees of freedom, as R's quantreg::summary.rq does; the interval is estimate \(\pm t_{n-p,(1+\text{level})/2}\) times the standard error.

    Members

    Fields

    namesCoefficient names, "(Intercept)" first when present.
    estimateThe point estimates \(\hat\beta_\tau\).
    std_errorEstimated standard errors.
    t_valueestimate / std_error.
    p_valueTwo-sided p-values for \(\beta_j=0\).
    lowerLower confidence limits.
    upperUpper confidence limits.
    methodThe method that produced the standard errors.
    levelThe confidence level of lower and upper.
    bandwidthThe bandwidth \(h\) for IID and NID (Hall and Sheather's, shrunk if \(\tau\pm h\) leaves (0, 1)); 0 for the bootstrap.
    replicationsBootstrap replications used; 0 for IID and NID.
    degrees_of_freedomResidual degrees of freedom \(n-p\).
