    struct datamunge::bayes

    INLAResult

    struct INLAResult
    #include <datamunge/bayes/inla.hpp> source

    Gaussian-mixture posterior summaries and the full retained hyperparameter grid. These are approximate moments, not exact posterior draws. Within variance is the weighted conditional variance; between variance is variance of conditional modes. Their sum is total variance; neither term alone orders total variance against EB. All members own their data. Conditional covariance storage costs O(K*p^2).

    Example
    dm::bayes::INLAResult r;
std::print("{}\n",r.theta_grid_weights.size()); // 0
    See also

    INLA::fit(), inla_latent_summary(), inla_grid_table()
