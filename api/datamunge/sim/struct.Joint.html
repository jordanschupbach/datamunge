    struct datamunge::sim

    Joint

    struct Joint
    #include <datamunge/sim/types.hpp> source

    A joint from a dynamic child to a parent body or the fixed world. Anchor/axis are in the child's rotated local frame; coordinate zero is its authored pose. Connected bodies do not collide. Each child has at most one joint.

    Example
    dm::sim::Joint joint; joint.child=1; joint.validate();

    typeHinge by default.
    childDynamic child; must be nonzero.
    parentParent body, or zero for the world.
    anchorChild-local offset in metres, ignoring shape dimensions.
    axisNonzero child-local generalized axis, normalized when attached.
    limitedWhether hard coordinate limits apply.
    limit_minLower limit, at most zero; hinges within -pi, sliders within -1000.
    limit_maxUpper limit, at least zero; hinges within pi, sliders within 1000.
    dampingNonnegative generalized viscous damping, at most 1000000.
    effort_limitMaximum motor force/torque; external impulses remain independent.
    velocity_limitMaximum requested motor rate; contact backdrive is not clamped.
    armatureReflected generalized inertia, kg m² (hinge) or kg (slider).
    frictionPassive dry joint friction, N m or N.
