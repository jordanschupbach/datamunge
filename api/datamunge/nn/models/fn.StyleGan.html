    StyleGan

    #include <datamunge/nn/style_gan.hpp>
    #source
    StyleGanOutput StyleGan(
    ParameterStore & generator,
    ParameterStore & critic,
    const Tensor & real,
    const Tensor & z,
    const Tensor & noise4,
    const Tensor & noise8,
    bool version2 = false)

    Evaluate either styled least-squares game with explicit latent/noise inputs and no fitting.

    Distinct same-dtype Float32/64 G/D stores; finite signed CPU real[N,8,8,1], z[M,8],noise4[M,4,4,1],noise8[M,8,8,1], N,M>0, strides allowed. Real pool count may differ; latent/noise rows must align. Map z first, synthesize with that same W at both layers, score real/fake separately. Source order/names initialize G661/17(AdaIN) or625/19(version2) values/leaves as documented by StyleGanSynthesize, then D493/6. Source seeds6607/6619; independent stores are required for the two variant trials. No hidden style mixing, random noise, truncation, W average, EMA or extra penalty. G is full fake-to1 MSE; D half the sum of real-to1/fake-to0 means. All applicable first/higher real,z,noise and parameter paths remain live; G gradients pass through current D.

    No capture, optimizer, hidden no-grad, training loop or variant state. Caller captures D/steps D then makes a fresh G forward through updated D with the same supplied latent/noise batch and steps G alone. Existing leaves/inputs unchanged; only missing parameters initialize, no later RNG. Owning fields retain graph/storage. Validate stores/all inputs before leaves; stored-leaf/ math errors may follow initialization. O(N+M) fixed-map work/storage, including per-example modulated kernels for version2; synchronize shared stores.

    Parameters
    generator

    Distinct matching floating G store, source seed6607.

    critic

    Distinct matching floating D store, source seed6619.

    real

    Finite signed matching CPU[N,8,8,1] observed images, positive N.

    z

    Finite signed matching CPU[M,8] latent bank, positive M.

    noise4

    Explicit finite signed matching CPU[M,4,4,1] spatial noise.

    noise8

    Explicit finite signed matching CPU[M,8,8,1] spatial noise.

    version2

    True selects modulated/demodulated synthesis; false(default) selects AdaIN.

    Returns

    Owning StyleGanOutput with ten complete live source fields.

    Throws
    std::invalid_argument

    For invalid/aliased stores, inputs, products, leaves or nonfinite loss.

    std::logic_error

    For non-CPU storage.

    Example
    #include <dm/dm.hpp>
namespace n=dm::nn;n::ParameterStore g(6607),d(6619);
auto
o=n::models::StyleGan(g,d,n::zeros({2,8,8,1}),n::ones({2,8}),n::zeros({2,4,4,1}),n::zeros({2,8,8,1}));
auto gradient=n::grad(o.d_loss,d.parameters());
n::OptimizerConfig c;c.kind=n::OptimizerKind::Adam;c.beta1=.5;
n::Optimizer adam(d,.0002,c);adam.step(gradient); // caller then makes a fresh G forward.
    References

    • Karras,Laine and Aila (2018/2019), https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.04948 ; Karras et al. (2019/2020), https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04958 . These are compact fixed8x8 teaching variants with least-squares objectives, not full paper training.
