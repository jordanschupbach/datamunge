    ComputusGregorianTrace

    struct ComputusGregorianTrace
    #include <datamunge/algorithms/computus.hpp> source

    Owned actual Meeus/Jones/Butcher integer steps and the resulting date. All divisions are floors of nonnegative integers for supported positive years. a is a zero-based Metonic index; the golden number is a+1. h is a nominal lunar offset, not an unconditional observed or corrected full-moon date. l+1 is the nominal following-Sunday offset. m subtracts one week in the two exceptional Sunday cases. No iteration, replay, allocation or shared state.

    Example
    const auto t=dm::algorithms::computus_gregorian_trace(1954);
std::print("{} {} {} {} {}\n",t.a,t.h,t.l,t.m,t.date.day); // 16 28 6 1 18
