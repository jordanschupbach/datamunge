#+title: Christofides' Metric TSP Algorithm
#+options: toc:t num:t
#+property: header-args:c++ :eval no
* Mathematics
For a metric complete graph, take an MST \(T\), a minimum perfect matching
\(M\) on the odd-degree vertices, an Euler tour of \(T\cup M\), then shortcut
repeated vertices.  \(w(T)\le OPT\), \(w(M)\le OPT/2\), and the triangle
inequality makes shortcutting non-increasing, hence \(w(C)\le3OPT/2\).
* Pseudocode
#+begin_example
MST; exact minimum matching of odd vertices
Euler circuit of their multigraph
shortcut repeats and close the Hamiltonian tour
#+end_example
This implementation uses exact bitmask matching, limiting the odd set to 22.
* C++ example
