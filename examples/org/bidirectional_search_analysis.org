#+title: Bidirectional Search
#+options: toc:t num:t
#+property: header-args:c++ :eval no
* Mathematics and algorithm
Two BFS waves grow from start and goal in an undirected unweighted graph.
When their visited sets meet, joining predecessor chains gives a shortest
path.  If branching factor is \(b\) and distance \(d\), searching two halves
visits about \(2b^{d/2}\) states rather than \(b^d\).
#+begin_example
initialize queues and predecessor maps at both endpoints
alternate frontier expansion until a cross-visited edge appears
join the two predecessor chains at the meeting point
#+end_example
