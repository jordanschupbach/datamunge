* Mathematics
A* expands minimum \(f(v)=g(v)+h(v)\), where \(g\) is discovered path cost
and admissible \(h\le h^*\) lower-bounds remaining cost.  When a goal leaves
the queue, every competing frontier path has lower bound at least its cost,
proving optimality.  Consistency prevents closed-node reopening.
#+begin_example
g[start]=0; queue by g+h
pop best; stop if goal
relax outgoing edges and record predecessors
#+end_example
Binary-heap graph search is \(O((V+E)\log V)\).
