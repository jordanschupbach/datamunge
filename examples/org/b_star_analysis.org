#+options: toc:t num:t
#+property: header-args:c++ :eval no
* Mathematics
Here B* follows the list's graph-search meaning: find the least-cost member of
a goal set.  A lower bound \(h(v)\) estimates cost to the nearest goal, and
the frontier is ordered by \(g+h\).  If \(h\) is admissible for the goal set,
the first removed goal is globally cheapest by the same lower-bound proof as
A*.
#+begin_example
mark every allowed goal
best-first search from start by path-cost + goal-set lower bound
stop at the first removed goal
#+end_example
