#+title: Warnsdorff's Knight-Tour Rule
#+options: toc:t num:t
#+property: header-args:c++ :eval no
* Mathematics
At each square, choose the legal unvisited move with smallest onward degree.
This fail-first heuristic consumes constrained squares before they become
unreachable.  It is not a proof of existence on every board, but is remarkably
effective; deterministic row/column tie-breaking completes the standard 8x8.
#+begin_example
mark start
repeat until board is full:
  enumerate legal unvisited knight moves
  choose one having fewest legal onward moves
  fail if none exists; otherwise mark it
#+end_example
Direct degree checks cost \(O(RC)\).
