#+title: Euclidean Minimum Spanning Tree
#+options: toc:t num:t
#+property: header-args:c++ :eval no

#+begin_abstract
The Euclidean MST connects planar points with minimum total segment length.
We reduce the complete geometric graph to the tested Kruskal implementation,
give pseudocode, and solve the unit square.
#+end_abstract

* Mathematics and algorithm

For points \(p_i=(x_i,y_i)\), edge weight is
\[
w_{ij}=\sqrt{(x_i-x_j)^2+(y_i-y_j)^2}.
\]
The cut property makes the lightest edge crossing any component cut safe.

#+begin_example
construct every pair (i,j,distance(p_i,p_j))
sort edges by distance
use union-find to accept exactly cycle-free component joins
stop after n-1 edges
#+end_example

This direct form costs \(O(n^2\log n)\) time and \(O(n^2)\) space; Delaunay
triangulation can reduce the candidate edge set.

* C++ example
