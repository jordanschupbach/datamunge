    Gamma-Poisson updating

    Abstract

    Simulate counts over windows of different lengths at a common event rate. Fit a Poisson model whose expected count is rate times window length. A Gamma(2, 1) shape-rate prior updates by the total observed count and exposure. Quadrature of the individual Poisson likelihood terms checks the exact fit. Integrating the rate out gives a negative binomial prediction for a new window.
