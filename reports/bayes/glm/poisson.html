    Poisson log-linear regression

    Abstract

    Counts have mean and variance exp(intercept + slope × predictor). Exponentiating the slope gives the multiplicative change in the expected count per unit of the predictor. The trace plot shows the first 250 retained slope draws from each chain.

    Reproducible analysis

    Slope 0.55, 90% interval [0.33, 0.78], split R-hat 1.000, ESS 1695. Post-warmup divergences: 0.

    Reference

    Stan User’s Guide 2.19, chapters 1 and 5. Stan User’s Guide 2.19 PDF
