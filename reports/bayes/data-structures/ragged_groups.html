    Partial pooling for ragged groups

    Abstract

    Groups have different observation counts. A flat response array and cumulative offsets identify their rows. Each latent group mean has a Normal population prior with SD one. Integrating out the group means yields a Gaussian likelihood for each group average with variance 1 + 1/n, giving greater influence to more precise groups. The population mean has a Normal(0, 3) prior.

    Reproducible analysis

    Posterior mean 1.884, posterior SD 0.675, edge mass 1.25e-20.

    Reference

    Stan User’s Guide 2.19, section 8.2. Stan User’s Guide 2.19 PDF
