    Fitting and querying a discrete Bayesian network

    Abstract

    Simulate a binary treatment and outcome with a shared cause. Fit conditional probability tables using a Dirichlet prior and compare observational conditioning to an intervention. Fit a Chow–Liu tree to the same data to explore an alternative probabilistic factorization.

    Conditioning on treatment changes the distribution of the shared cause. Fixing treatment by intervention preserves the shared cause's marginal distribution and replaces the treatment mechanism. The generating contrast under intervention is 0.2. The Chow–Liu tree chooses edges by empirical mutual information and orients them from a chosen root for factorization.
