    Discovering a Gaussian graph with PC-stable

    Abstract

    Simulate two independent causes of a shared downstream variable. Fit a partially directed graph and use conditional independence tests to see how a collider changes the relationship between its parents.

    Recover the collider from observations

    Partial correlations before and after conditioning: -0.0145577 and -0.515408. Independence tests: 36.

    The simulated arrows are A and B into Collider, Collider into Downstream, and A into its neighbor. The last edge can stay undirected in the learned graph because its direction is ambiguous within the observational equivalence class. The search assumes independent Gaussian observations, causal sufficiency, acyclicity and faithfulness. The test cutoff and conditioning depth can affect the recovered graph.
