    Estimating treatment effects from observational data

    Abstract

    Simulate a study where a baseline covariate influences both treatment and outcome. Use an assumed DAG to select an adjustment covariate, then compare g-computation, inverse probability weighting and augmented weighting.

    Identify an adjustment set and estimate the effect

    Adjustment node 0. Estimated propensity range 0.0443045 to 0.960241.

    Raw 3.35464, g-computation 2.09618, IPW 2.07535, AIPW 2.03982.

    The horizontal line marks the generating effect of 2. G-computation averages outcome predictions for both treatment states over the same covariates. IPW balances the groups by their fitted treatment probabilities and AIPW combines a propensity model with the outcome predictions. These are point estimates under consistency, positivity and no unmeasured confounding. Extreme fitted propensities are a warning that some comparisons have little support.
