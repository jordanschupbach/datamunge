    Instrumental variables and difference-in-differences

    Abstract

    Fit two quasi-experimental estimators to simulated data. A randomized encouragement shifts a confounded exposure, and a panel intervention shifts the treated group after the baseline period.

    Fit 2SLS and a two-period panel estimate

    First-stage F = 156.461. 2SLS = 2.08942. Panel DiD = 2.99883.

    The instrument is independent of the confounder and affects the outcome through exposure alone. The first-stage partial F measures relevance in the sample. The panel simulation has parallel trends under no treatment, with a baseline level gap between groups. With two periods, parallel trends is an assumption about the unobserved post-treatment counterfactual.
