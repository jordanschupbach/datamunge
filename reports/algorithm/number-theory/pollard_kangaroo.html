    Kangaroo logarithms: actual seeded jumps and exact interval distances

    Abstract

    Study the actual native producer from a tiny, inspectable example through the retained original experiment and explicit boundary/work controls. Complete editable C++ programs own every rendered native figure.

    Mechanism and scope

    The native algorithm precomputes a seeded32-entry jump table, walks a complete tame trail from g^b, then walks the target until a trail lookup supplies a candidate. Collisions can include cyclic-group aliases, so every candidate is range-checked and verified by modular powering. Failure after finite attempts does not show that no logarithm exists, and a five-setting plot does not prove a square-root work law. Exact widened distances preserve b+distance near the uint64 limit. The original three secrets and five interval widths remain; no dataset is needed.

    \[g^{b+d_T}=h g^{d_W},\qquad x=b+d_T-d_W,\qquad a\le x\le b.\]
