    Guided backpropagation:unchanged logits and a modified backward rule

    Abstract

    Gradient-based image visualizations can change when the backward propagation rule changes. Freeze the verified MNIST classifier and compare ordinary derivatives with a positive-positive guided rule,including every sign/zero gate and real-image target/weight controls.

    Source example: examples/cpp/source/nn/1_convolutional_neural_networks/1.11_interpretability/guided_backprop.cpp.

    The method

    At a ReLU with pre-activation a and incoming signal g,the guided rule returns g*1[a>0]*1[g>0]. Forward ReLU values remain identical. Because the extra gate depends on the incoming signal,the rule is nonlinear in g and is not the derivative VJP of that forward function.

    \[G(a,g)=g\,1[a>0]1[g>0].\]

    No classifier fitting occurs here. Restore the exact selected and actual-initial arrays produced by the explicit tensor-Adam loop in vanilla_saliency;all model computations and control statistics remain tensors.

    The concept diagram below is drawn by dm::graphics::Diagram. Its editable C++ source is the complete block below; regeneration uses the same datamunge toolchain as the numerical examples. Exports consume the saved local SVG.

We first show the exact gate rule and why it is not a linear vector-Jacobian product,then inspect fixed-target real-image signals.

    Hand-checkable positive/negative/zero gates

    Ordinary ReLU reverse propagation is g*1[x>0]. Guided propagation additionally requires g>0. Incoming-only deconvolution gating omits the forward x>0test. Atx2,the true derivative of a weighted ReLU with incoming-3is-3,but guided returns0. For incoming-3and4,separate guided signals sum to4while the guided signal of their sum1is1. A derivative VJP must be linear in the incoming vector; this rule is not. Finite differences therefore check the ordinary derivative and expose the difference;they do not certify guided propagation as a derivative.

    Conclusion

    All forward predictions remain unchanged while the visualization signal changes. Exact sign/zero checks expose a nonlinear modified backward rule;real-image target and weight controls assess dependence without turning the guided signal into an ordinary derivative or causal explanation.
