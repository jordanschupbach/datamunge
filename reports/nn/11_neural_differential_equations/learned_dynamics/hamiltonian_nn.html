    Hamiltonian neural network for pendulum dynamics

    Hamiltonian neural network for pendulum dynamics

    A scalar network represents the Hamiltonian as a function of pendulum angle and momentum. The time derivative of angle is the Hamiltonian’s momentum derivative, and the time derivative of momentum is its negative angle derivative. Training observes the vector field at sampled states. It learns the energy indirectly, up to an additive constant.

    The experiment fits 2,048 states and tests on 256 new states. It then integrates the fitted field with RK4 from twelve initial conditions. The learned Hamiltonian is conserved by the continuous model equations. Numerical integration can introduce small errors. The known pendulum energy may drift along the fitted trajectories because the learned vector field is approximate.
