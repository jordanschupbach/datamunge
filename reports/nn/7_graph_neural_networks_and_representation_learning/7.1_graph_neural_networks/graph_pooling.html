    DiffPool, TopK, and SAGPool

    DiffPool, TopK, and SAGPool

    All three methods reduce each eight-node weighted ring to four nodes. DiffPool learns a soft assignment of original nodes to clusters and coarsens both features and edges with that assignment. Link reconstruction and assignment entropy regularize fitting. TopK ranks nodes with a normalized learned projection. SAGPool ranks them after neighbor aggregation. Both hard-selection methods keep an induced subgraph and multiply retained features by a learned score gate.

    A shared classifier uses the pooled features and pooled neighbor means. All variants see the same 128 training graphs and 64 held-out graphs. The two classes differ in whether positive and negative signals cluster or alternate around the ring, with node IDs randomly shuffled.
