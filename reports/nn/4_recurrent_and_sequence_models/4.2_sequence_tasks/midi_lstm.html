    Generate monophonic MIDI with an LSTM

    Generate monophonic MIDI with an LSTM

    A token describes one of twelve pitches or a rest, each with an eighth-note or quarter-note duration. The seeded corpus follows short transposed motifs with rhythmic variation, rests, and occasional pitch changes. All 384 phrases are distinct. The split holds out 64 for validation and another 64 for test.

    A 16-unit LSTM predicts the next event from a one-hot encoding of the current event. During fitting it reads the observed prefix and minimizes next-token negative log likelihood. During generation it starts with middle C and feeds its own sampled event back into the same recurrent cell. A held-out phrase checks that an unbroken pass and one-step generation agree when given the same prefix. The generated track preserves both pitch and timing.

    Epoch 1 validation NLL 2.902171 Epoch 2 validation NLL 2.583169 Epoch 3 validation NLL 2.394058 Test NLL 3.260194 → 2.374941. Streaming maximum error 0. Wrote 59 notes and 6 rests, including the prompt, to build/generated.mid.

    The likelihood measures how well the model predicts events in these synthetic motifs. The piano roll shows one sampled continuation. The file uses a single track at 120 beats per minute and 480 ticks per quarter note.
