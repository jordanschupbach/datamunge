    Direct LSTM and GRU forecasts with chronological evaluation

    Abstract

    Fit two recurrent models to the same seasonal series. At each forecast origin, the preceding 24 observations produce all six forecast leads. Compare the learned forecasts with persistence and a 24-step seasonal baseline.

    Form complete target windows inside each split

    The series combines a period-24 cycle, slow amplitude modulation, and period-12 and period-73 oscillations. Fit on complete targets before index 768, validate through index 959, and test through index 1151. Compute the normalization mean and standard deviation on the first 768 observations. In the rolling test, each forecast can use observations that have already arrived, including earlier test observations. A perturbation control checks that future observations leave the first test forecast unchanged.

    Fit both models

    A 12-unit LSTM or GRU summarizes each history. Its last state feeds one linear layer with six outputs. Each model trains for three epochs with Adam and mean squared error across all leads. The setup mirrors the upstream experiment using Datamunge’s native cell and optimizer implementations.

    Compare test forecasts by lead

    The first panel shows the first 72 rolling one-step forecasts. The second uses every test origin to compare RMSE by lead. The seasonal baseline is strong because the generator has a prominent, fixed period of 24. In this run both fitted models have lower RMSE than the seasonal baseline at every lead. At lead one the LSTM gives 0.145 and the GRU gives 0.105, compared with 0.253 for the seasonal baseline.
