    Deep & Cross networks for click prediction

    Deep & Cross networks for click prediction

    Embed the user segment and item topic, and concatenate their embeddings with a numerical context feature. The deep branch passes this base vector through two ReLU layers. The cross branch applies two residual crossings, each using the same original base vector. A linear head combines both branches to predict click logits.

    DCN multiplies the base vector by a scalar projection of the current cross state, then adds a bias and the residual. DCN-v2 multiplies the base vector elementwise by a full affine projection of the current cross state, then adds the residual. The full projection lets each coordinate learn its own combination of cross-state features.

    The simulator includes segment-by-topic interactions and a quadratic context effect. Hold out every row from pairs whose segment and topic IDs sum to a multiple of eight. Also hold out four rows from each remaining pair. Compare the two kinds of held-out data after each epoch. Both models use Datamunge initialization and the upstream Adam learning rate, batch size, and number of epochs. On this split, the fitted DCN gets worse on unseen pairs even though its seen-pair loss improves.

    Set DM_DEEP_CROSS_REFERENCE to the upstream output/models/deep_cross directory to replay both upstream checkpoints and compare intermediate states and logits.
