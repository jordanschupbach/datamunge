    Laplace approximation for Bayesian logistic regression

    Abstract

    Fit Bayesian logistic regression to simulated binary outcomes, then approximate the coefficient posterior by a Gaussian at its mode. Compare posterior-averaged probabilities with predictions at the mode.

    Use curvature at the posterior mode

    Generate 48 binary observations with an intercept and two continuous features. The prior on each coefficient is standard Gaussian. Newton updates minimize the negative log posterior. The mode gives the Gaussian center, and the inverse Hessian at the mode gives the covariance.

    Use a Cholesky factor to transform 4,096 standard Gaussian coefficient draws. Average the logistic probabilities across draws and compare with the result from applying the logistic transform to the mode. The x-axis follows a single slice of the two-dimensional predictor space, with the second feature equal to −0.5 times the first. The curve includes extrapolation outside the fitting range.
