    Per-example clipping and Gaussian noise in DP-SGD

    Per-example clipping and Gaussian noise in DP-SGD

    A logistic classifier has one gradient vector per training row. Clip each vector to Euclidean norm 0.5 before summing them. Add Gaussian noise with standard deviation 0.75 to each coordinate of the sum, then divide by the batch size to obtain the SGD update.

    The experiment uses synthetic rows and public random seeds so its 24 updates can be inspected exactly. This setup demonstrates the clipping and noise calculations. A privacy guarantee requires a specified private-data sampling mechanism, secret randomness, and privacy accounting. The plot shows the largest raw and clipped gradient norms at each step.

    24 updates. Noise standard deviation on the sum 0.75. Held-out correct 251/256.
