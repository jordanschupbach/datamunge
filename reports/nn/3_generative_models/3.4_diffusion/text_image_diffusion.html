    Text-conditioned diffusion for a compositional image vocabulary

    Abstract

    A small denoising network learns to draw bars from two-word prompts. Learned embeddings encode position and orientation separately. Deterministic DDIM runs all four combinations from matched noise so the effect of changing either word is visible in the fitted samples.

    Fit a clean-image predictor

    The synthetic data consist of 8×8 images with either a short horizontal bar or a taller vertical bar. The first word selects the left or right column and each clean image receives a random vertical shift. At step t, add Gaussian noise to the scaled image with cumulative signal power ᾱ(t) = exp(−6(t/24)²). The denoiser receives the result, normalized time and its square, and a learned embedding for each word slot. It minimizes mean squared error against the original pixels. All four prompts appear during training, so the experiment tests whether changing words steers generation within the fitted vocabulary.

    Compare prompts under matched noise

    Each row of the gallery starts with one noise image repeated across four prompts. The deterministic DDIM step clips the clean prediction, infers the noise consistent with that clipped image, and carries it to the previous step without drawing another random sample. The report preserves the upstream architecture, schedule, training budget and noise seeds and uses Datamunge’s native parameter initialization and Adam implementation.

    Inspect the fitted samples and objective

    Columns show left vertical, left horizontal, right vertical and right horizontal. The template check compares each sample with the twelve clean bar patterns, allowing either orientation to shift vertically.
