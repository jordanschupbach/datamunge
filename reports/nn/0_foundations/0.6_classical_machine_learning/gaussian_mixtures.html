    Gaussian Mixtures: Responsibilities, Adam and EM

    Abstract

    Unlabelled observations may come from overlapping populations with different means and covariances. We inspect soft membership in a symmetric two-Gaussian model, then track explicit tensor Adam on a small separated fixture. A softplus variance bound prevents singular covariances, while likelihood and posterior normalization remain explicit checks.

    The method

    A mixture sums weighted Gaussian densities; the negative log of that sum is the observed-data objective. Here the learned covariance is diagonal (one scalar per component in the 1-D example). Unconstrained mass logits a and scale logits s define valid probabilities and positive variances:

    \[\pi=\operatorname{softmax}(a),\quad v=0.01+\operatorname{softplus}(s),\quad J=-\frac1N\sum_i\operatorname{logsumexp}_k\left[ \log\pi_k-\frac12\left(\log(2\pi v_k)+\frac{(x_i-\mu_k)^2}{v_k}\right)\right].\]

    Bayes responsibilities are the softmax of the component log-joints. Differentiating J weights each component score by these responsibilities, allowing the visible Adam loop to fit means, variances and masses directly. This is direct likelihood optimisation, with O(NKD) work per step, not EM. Adam does not guarantee monotonic likelihood. We check finite improvement and valid probabilities rather than require every step to improve. The variance bound is approached smoothly; it is not an eigenvalue-clipping update. The objective is nonconvex and labels can permute.

    Full-covariance EM

    The upstream fixture draws three groups with different masses and correlated coordinates. Initialize with k-means++ centers, then alternate posterior membership estimates and responsibility-weighted updates. An eigenvalue floor keeps each fitted covariance positive definite. The likelihood plotted below is the observed-data likelihood at each accepted state.

    EM accepted 7 updates: training likelihood -5727.651 to -2349.227. Held-out mean log density -2.431879; direct error 3.55e-15.

    Conclusion

    The symmetric posterior agrees with its analytic sigmoid. Direct tensor Adam improves the six-point observed-data likelihood while preserving normalised masses and the variance bound. On the correlated fixture, full-covariance EM recovers all three groups and agrees with direct held-out density calculations.
