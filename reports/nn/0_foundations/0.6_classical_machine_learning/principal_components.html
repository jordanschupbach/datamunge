    Principal Components: Variance, Projection and Kernel Centring

    Abstract

    Correlated measurements can be represented in fewer orthogonal coordinates, but projection must use a fixed training centre and scale. We recover the known axes of four points, check variance lost by a rank-one reconstruction and check linear Gram and covariance spectra. A built-in Iris example then illustrates descriptive covariance geometry.

    The method

    After centring the data matrix X, its thin SVD X=USVᵀ gives orthonormal axes V and scores US. The sample covariance eigenvalues are s_j²/(N-1). Keeping q leading components minimises squared reconstruction error over rank-q orthogonal projections:

    \[\|X-XV_qV_q^T\|_F^2=\sum_{j>q}s_j^2.\]

    The report fits a unit direction with explicit tensor SGD and a two-axis subspace with explicit tensor Adam. In the latter, differentiable QR maps unconstrained weights to orthonormal columns at every step; QR does not supply the data-dependent optimum. The reconstruction loss is that of a tied linear autoencoder. SVD is used only to check the final minimum. Learned axes may rotate within the same principal subspace, so compare reconstruction error rather than individual vector signs. Each objective evaluation costs O(NDq), plus O(Dq²) for its QR constraint.

    The optimum follows by rotating into V coordinates, where the Frobenius norm separates into squared column norms. Scaling predictors changes covariance PCA to correlation PCA; the toy uses unscaled data, while Iris uses tensor-computed sample standard deviations. Kernel PCA instead centres a Gram matrix K with H=I-11ᵀ/N and diagonalises HKH. Its eigenvalues are Gram eigenvalues, not sample variances; divide by N-1 to compare with covariance PCA. Query centring must use training means, not each query batch's own mean. No input-space inverse is claimed for kernel PCA.

    Simple examples

    Four points (±2,0),(0,±1) have zero mean and diagonal sample covariance (8/3,2/3). Keeping only x loses total squared error 2.

    Learned unit axis [1, 5.68448261274509e-37]; reconstruction SSE 2.000000000; SVD sample variances [2.6666666666666647, 0.6666666666666669].

    Checking the theory

    Linear Gram eigenvalues [0, 0, 1.9999999999999998, 7.999999999999999]; covariance eigenvalues [0.6666666666666666, 2.6666666666666665]; RBF query-centering error 0.000000000000.

    A dataset example

    The 150 Iris rows have four predictors. Tensor preprocessing uses their sample standard deviations; no species label enters fitting. QR-normalised weights span the two learned axes, which can rotate within the optimal subspace.

    Learned reconstruction SSE 24.953285088; SVD oracle 24.953285088; orthogonality 0.000000000000; batching error 0.000000000000.

    Whitening and held-out reconstruction

    The upstream linear fixture rotates three independent directions and translates the result. Their population variances are 9, 1 and 0.04. Whitening divides the retained scores by the square roots of the sample variances, so their sample covariance is the identity. Reconstructing with two axes leaves the last direction's energy in the residual.

    Whitened covariance error 1.55e-15. Rank-two reconstruction MSE 0.013333333 on train and 0.013333333 on held-out rows.

    Kernel PCA on concentric rings

    Fit a three-axis RBF embedding to two rings. The training-centred Gram matrix should have zero row sums and nonnegative eigenvalues to rounding. Projection of the training rows should reproduce their eigen-embedding, and the coordinates of a new query should be independent of other queries in the batch.

    Retained eigenvalues [13.911981435228848, 8.682219658615036, 8.682219658615029]. Centering 3.00e-15, eigen residual 1.55e-15, reprojection 1.50e-15, query-batch error 0.00e+00.

    Conclusion

    The known covariance and discarded-variance identity agree on the four-point example. Fixed training centring makes projections independent of query batching. PCA describes variance, not necessarily class separation, and kernel coordinates do not provide an input-space pre-image.
