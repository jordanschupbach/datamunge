    Forecast with overlapping PatchTST history tokens

    Abstract

    Train a univariate PatchTST encoder to forecast six observations. Inspect attention over the seven patches and compare the forecast with held-out observations.

    Length-six windows advance by three samples through the 24 observed values. A learned projection and position embedding turn each window into a token. One encoder block combines two-head self-attention and a GELU feed-forward layer with residual connections and layer normalization. The linear head reads all seven tokens to predict the six leads directly. Nie et al., A Time Series is Worth 64 Words

    The history contains only observations before the forecast origin. Attention can look across the entire observed history. Scaling uses the fitting prefix, and training, validation, and test targets occupy disjoint intervals. The initialization fixture matches the upstream run.

    The final validation MSE was 0.022483. Test RMSE fell from 1.192475 to 0.153591 across 187 origins.

    The attention panel shows how both heads distribute the newest query patch's weight among the seven observed key patches at the first test origin. The forecast panel uses the original data scale.
