    Gaussian cross-entropy policy search

    Gaussian cross-entropy policy search

    A bounded feedback controller combines position, velocity, and a bias into a force with a hyperbolic tangent. Each rollout starts at one of twelve mirrored initial states and runs for 24 steps. Penalize displacement, velocity, and effort with squared costs weighted by 1, 0.1, and 0.025, respectively. Average the negative total cost across starts. The report also saves all pre-transition states for the final mean policy.

    Sample 48 controllers from a diagonal Gaussian, evaluate them on the same initial states, and retain the 10 highest-return controllers. Fit the next Gaussian to these elites using maximum-likelihood mean and variance. Mix 80 percent of the refit into the previous distribution and apply a variance floor. Evaluate the mean controller after each update on held-out starts, without sampling policy weights.

    Rubinstein and Kroese (2004)
