    Sparse-grid exploration schedule

    Sparse-grid exploration schedule

    This fixture gives an exploration algorithm 2,048 actions on a seven-by-seven grid. Actions move up, down, left or right, with the current coordinate clipped to the grid boundary. The agent starts in the upper-left cell and receives a task reward of one on entering the lower-right cell. An episode resets there or after 64 actions.

    Two tabular learners share every transition. The behavior table adds half the supplied curiosity reward and chooses epsilon-greedy actions with exploration probability one quarter. The task table sees only the goal reward and supplies the greedy evaluation policy. Every sixteen actions, the training callback receives 32 transitions sampled with replacement from replay. It uses a random stream separate from action selection. An episode reset callback lets stateful curiosity modules clear episodic state while retaining their learned parameters.

    This example compares task-only training with a constant bonus callback to illustrate the schedule. A constant bonus rewards visits uniformly. See random network distillation for a predictor that gives different bonuses to different observations.
