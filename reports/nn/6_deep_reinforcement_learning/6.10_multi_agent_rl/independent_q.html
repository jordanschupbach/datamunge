    Independent Q-learning with a shared reward

    Independent Q-learning with a shared reward

    Two agents move along separate five-cell chains. Each policy observes its own position. Both receive a reward only when both agents have arrived at their absorbing goals. Each learner updates its own state-action table with a bootstrap from its private successor.

    The training schedule uses more exploration in the first 128 episodes, then reduces the random-action probability. The plot tracks joint arrivals during training. The final test runs the greedy policies from every ordered pair of nonterminal starting positions.

    3128 / 128
