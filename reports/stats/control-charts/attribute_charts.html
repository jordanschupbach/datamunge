    Attribute control charts for defects and defective units

    Abstract

    These synthetic inspections distinguish a defect count, where a unit can have more than one defect, from a count of defective units, where each inspected unit contributes at most one. The first eight observations fit each baseline. The last four are shown against those limits. c and np use constant opportunity, while u and p allow the denominators to vary.

    Interpret the chart

    The u-chart divides the defect count by exposure, allowing fractional exposure such as inspected area. The p-chart divides the defective count by units inspected. The p and np panels treat the same defective count sequence as two inspection designs, one with recorded varying sample sizes and one with exactly 100 units each. The p and u centers pool baseline numerators and denominators. Compare the regular and Laney limits. The Laney scale comes from adjacent standardized baseline residuals, so its limits can widen or narrow relative to the usual model. The red points apply the single-point limit rule.
