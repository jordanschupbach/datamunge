    Individuals and moving-range charts

    Abstract

    A synthetic production line records one fill weight per batch. The first ten weights establish the baseline. Later batches contain a level shift. An individuals chart tracks fill weight, and the moving-range chart tracks absolute differences between successive batches.

    Interpret the chart

    The individuals chart uses the baseline mean and estimates process standard deviation from the mean baseline moving range divided by the normal range factor for pairs. A moving range is indexed by the second observation in its pair, so its first point is batch two. A lasting level shift can leave the individuals chart outside its limits even after the adjacent ranges return to their baseline scale.
