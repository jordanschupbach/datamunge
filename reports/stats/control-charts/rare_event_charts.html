    Rare-event g and t charts

    Abstract

    Two synthetic event logs track the same kind of process in different units. The g-chart records the count of event-free opportunities between events. The t-chart records positive time intervals. Eight initial gaps fit each baseline, then the logs contain an unusually long gap followed by closely spaced events.

    Fit, monitor and plot

    ChartGapStatisticLowerUpper
    g-chart10145.0000.000117.000
    t-chart1048.0000.00736.342
    t-chart110.0010.00736.342

    Interpret the chart

    Each center line is the fitted median. The limits use the fitted geometric or exponential tails, rather than a symmetric band around the center. The integer lower limit can land at zero, so a zero gap on this g-chart is on the limit and does not signal under the strict outside-limit rule. The continuous t-chart can flag unusually small positive gaps.
