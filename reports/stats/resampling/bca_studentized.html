    Bootstrap percentile, BCa and t-pivot intervals

    Abstract

    Simulate positive, right-skewed service times. Compare three bootstrap confidence intervals for the same mean. The studentized method receives the mean's sample standard-error estimator as a callback and recomputes it within each resample.

    Data and analysis

    Observed mean 1.535. Discarded zero-SE resamples: 0.

    IntervalLowerUpper
    Percentile0.9452.304
    BCa1.0352.511
    Bootstrap-t0.9773.310

    Interpretation

    BCa changes the percentile positions using an empirical bias correction and leave-one-out acceleration. Bootstrap-t estimates the quantiles of the studentized pivot and inverts them on the original mean scale. Both intervals can be asymmetric. Their widths differ even though both runs start with the same seed and observe the same sample.
