    Friedman, Kendall’s W and Page’s ordered test

    Abstract

    Three known dose levels are applied to each simulated subject. A shared subject intercept produces large between-subject variation. The tests use one row per subject. Dose order is fixed before observing the outcomes.

    Data and analysis

    Friedman Q 3.583, p 0.1667. Kendall W 0.075. Page L 293.0, one-sided p 0.2352.

    Interpretation

    Friedman detects differences of any ordering. Kendall's W expresses the same rank-sum variation as concordance on a zero-to-one scale. Page instead gives greater weight to higher doses and tests the prespecified increasing trend. The references here are chi-square for Friedman and normal for Page. Kendall tau addresses paired association as a separate question.

    References

    • R Friedman rank test
    • Page, Ordered Hypotheses for Multiple Treatments
