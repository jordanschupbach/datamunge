    Mood median, Kruskal–Wallis and van der Waerden tests

    Abstract

    Simulate three independent groups with a common spread and separated locations. All three tests use the observed groups, with different reductions of the data. A separate rank-sum comparison contrasts the first and last groups as a specified comparison.

    Simulate, test and plot

    MethodStatisticp-value
    Mood median test (Pearson chi-square)0.2860.8669
    Kruskal-Wallis rank sum test4.1610.1249
    van der Waerden normal scores test4.8570.08817
    Wilcoxon rank sum test with continuity correction271.0000.04831

    Interpretation

    Mood's test keeps only the side of the pooled median and discards any observations exactly on it. Kruskal–Wallis keeps pooled ranks, and van der Waerden uses Normal quantiles of those ranks. Similar distributional shapes support the location-shift interpretation in this simulation. The prespecified rank-sum comparison uses only groups A and C.
