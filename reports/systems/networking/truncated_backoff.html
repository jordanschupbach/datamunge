    Truncated backoff: windows, slots and retry limits

    Abstract

    Repeated colliding transmissions need different retry delays. Binary exponential backoff widens the candidate slot window, caps its exponent, then abandons a frame after a fixed collision count.

    The method

    For c≥0, W(c)=2^min(c,10). A caller fraction u∈[0,1) maps to slot floor(uW), so every slot occupies an interval of length1/W. The ideal mean is (W−1)/2 and two independent ideal uniform stations collide again with probability1/W. These are probability calculations, not network throughput measurements. At c≥16 the policy abandons the frame even though its descriptive window is still1024.

    Interpretation and limits

    Negative collision counts and nonfinite/out-of-range fractions throw before shifting or integer conversion. The caller owns randomness and frame state; the API does not simulate Ethernet traffic or authorize retries after abandonment.

    Every figure is saved locally by its complete editable program. No separate reconstruction supplies algorithm state. Counters describe the selected events, not elapsed time or peak memory.
